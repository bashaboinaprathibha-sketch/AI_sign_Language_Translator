About AI Sign Language Translator

The system is designed to assist communication between sign-language users and people who do not understand sign language through real-time artificial intelligence.

⚡

Real-Time Sign Recognition

Processes live video feeds with ultra-low latency (~35ms inference per frame) for smooth, natural interaction.

🖐

Camera-Based Hand Detection

Leverages MediaPipe to accurately track 21 3D spatial hand landmarks across varying illumination and backgrounds.

🤖

AI Gesture Classification

Hybrid intelligence utilizing Convolutional Neural Networks (CNN) for static signs and temporal analysis for sequential signs.

📝

Text Translation

Converts recognized gestures into grammatical, human-readable words, alphabet letters, numbers, and full sentences.

🔊

Text-to-Speech (TTS)

Browser-native voice synthesis powered by Web Speech API, voicing translations aloud with natural cadence.

🌐

Multiple Languages

Supports English, Hindi, Telugu, Tamil, Kannada, Malayalam, Spanish, and French with localized BCP-47 speech codes.

📋

Translation History

Maintains persistent session logs with recognized sign timestamps and confidence scores, exportable and clearable.

📱

Responsive Mobile Design

Flawlessly adapts to desktop monitors, laptops, tablets, and smartphones with mobile camera switching.

📊 Dataset & Training Information

Robust machine learning requires extensive dataset diversity across real-world variations.

✋ Hand Shapes

Captures variations in finger thickness, joint flexibility, and hand sizes across diverse demographics.

🔄 Hand Angles

Trained on yaw, pitch, and roll rotations (±25°) to guarantee recognition from imperfect camera angles.

📏 Distances from Camera

Normalizes hand scale from close-ups (0.3m) to standard desk seating distances (1.5m).

💡 Lighting Conditions

Images collected under natural sunlight, fluorescent office bulbs, dim rooms, and back-lit settings.

🖼️ Background Diversity

Hand segmentation tested against busy office spaces, living rooms, outdoor environments, and blank walls.

👥 Different Users

Incorporates recordings from multiple individuals to prevent biometric overfitting on single hands.

🧭 Gesture Orientations

Supports both left and right-handed signers with horizontal mirror invariance.

⚙️ Data Preprocessing Pipeline

Step-by-step transformation applied to every camera frame before neural inference.

1

Background Removal & Masking

Isolates the hand region of interest (ROI) to eliminate irrelevant visual background clutter.

2

Noise Reduction

Gaussian blur and bilateral filtering smooth camera sensor grain and high-frequency video artifacts.

3

Image Resizing (64 × 64)

Standardizes the extracted hand crop into fixed 64x64 pixel tensor dimensions for CNN layers.

4

Pixel Normalization

Rescales 8-bit integer RGB values [0, 255] into floating point range [0.0, 1.0] for gradient stability.

5

Hand & Palm Detection

Single-shot detector identifies hand presence and locates the wrist anchor point.

6

Landmark Extraction

Extracts 21 3D spatial coordinates across wrist, thumb, index, middle, ring, and pinky joints.

7

Geometric Feature Extraction

Calculates finger extension boolean states, Euclidean fingertip distances, and spatial orientation angles.
